What if Maya gets a question wrong?

Maya is trained on your specific service offerings, pricing rules, and service area. If she encounters anything she's not 100% sure about, she escalates to your team via SMS or Slack with full context — so a human always closes the gap. You stay in control.

Will it sound robotic? My customers will hate that.

Maya speaks the way your best CSR speaks — short, friendly, no jargon. We tune her tone to match your brand voice during setup. Most homeowners can't tell they're texting an AI, and frankly, they don't care: they care that someone answered fast.

How fast is setup? I don't have time for a 6-month integration.

Live in 48 hours. We need a 30-minute kickoff to map your service area, job types, and calendar. We provision a Twilio number (or port yours), connect to ServiceTitan / Housecall Pro / Jobber / Google Calendar, and Maya is taking texts that weekend.

What does it cost?

Less than one missed install per month. We price per booked appointment, so you only pay when Maya delivers a qualified, calendared lead. No retainer, no per-message fees, no surprise invoices. Exact pricing covered on the setup call.
