Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Maya / HeatBooker ("we," "us," or "our") uses cookies and similar technologies on heatbooker.com. By using our site you agree to the use of strictly necessary cookies. You can manage your preferences for optional cookies via the banner at the bottom of the page.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored in your browser when you visit a website. They help sites remember your preferences, understand how you use the site, and deliver a consistent experience. We also use localStorage (similar to cookies, stored in your browser) to remember your consent choice.

3. Cloudflare Web Analytics — Cookieless by Design

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand site traffic. Unlike Google Analytics, Cloudflare Web Analytics:

  • Sets no cookies in your browser
  • Collects no personal identifiers (no IP logging, no fingerprinting)
  • Does not track you across sites
  • Is fully compliant with GDPR, CCPA, and ePrivacy Directive without requiring consent

Because it is cookieless and privacy-preserving, we run Cloudflare Web Analytics regardless of your cookie preference.

4. Your Choices

You can change your cookie preferences at any time using the button below. Choosing "Necessary Only" will not affect site functionality — all content, forms, and demos remain fully available.

Your current preference is stored in your browser's localStorage under the key maya_cookie_consent. Clear your browser storage to reset.
