Local Coverage FAQ

Quick answers to the most common questions about multi-city setup.

Do I need a different number per city?

No. Most contractors use one number. Maya uses the inbound text's zip code to confirm service area. If you expand to multiple cities, we provision additional numbers — but you're not required to. One number handles everything until you want city-specific branding.

What if I service multiple zip codes?

Tell us your service boundary in the setup call. Maya learns it and politely routes out-of-area leads — she'll respond to those inquiries with something like "We don't cover that area, but here's how to find a contractor who does." No awkward hang-ups. No wasted follow-up on leads you can't close.
