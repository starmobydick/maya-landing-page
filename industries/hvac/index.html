HVAC-Specific Questions

The answers your HVAC business actually needs.

Does Maya handle emergency AC calls differently?
Yes. Maya detects urgency signals ("95 degrees," "AC is out," "no cooling") and merges the acknowledgment with the next qualifying question — no back-and-forth delay. She routes emergencies to your on-call tech automatically, ensuring the homeowner feels heard and gets help fast.
Can Maya qualify leads for AC replacement vs. repair?
Absolutely. She asks about unit age, last service date, and whether it's stopped working entirely. If the unit is 10+ years old and fully down, she flags it as a likely replacement conversation so your tech walks in prepared — and can quote accordingly.
Does it work with my ServiceTitan dispatch board?
Yes. Maya integrates directly with ServiceTitan and Housecall Pro — booked jobs appear on your dispatch board with the homeowner's name, address, job type, and Maya's qualification notes attached. Your techs see full context, not just a name and phone number.
