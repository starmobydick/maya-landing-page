The Key Insight: Most Emergency Leads Never Visit Your Website

Think about how a homeowner behaves when their AC fails at 10 PM on a 110°F Phoenix night. They don't think "let me find a contractor's website and use their chat widget." They Google "HVAC near me," see a phone number, and text it immediately.

ServiceTitan WebChat only captures that homeowner if they click through to your website, scroll to find the chat widget, and start a conversation. That's three friction points — each one a chance for them to bounce to a competitor.

Maya captures them at the very first text — before they visit anyone's website. The lead is qualified and booked in under 5 seconds. The job is yours before your competitor even knows there was a lead.

The Full Picture

They're Complementary, Not Competing

The smartest contractors don't choose one or the other — they deploy both to cover every possible lead channel.

Use Maya For:

  • Direct texters from Google Ads
    Homeowners who click "call" or "text" from a Google Local Services ad — they skip your website entirely.
  • Yard sign and truck wrap leads
    Neighbors who see your number in the driveway and text it directly — no website involved.
  • Referral texts
    "My neighbor used you last year — can you come look at my AC?" texts sent to your number.
  • All after-hours emergencies
    2 AM pipe bursts and AC failures when no one is staffing ServiceTitan WebChat.

ServiceTitan WebChat For:

  • Organic website visitors
    Homeowners who find your website via SEO, review sites, or direct search and prefer to stay on the page.
  • Research-phase shoppers
    Homeowners comparing options, reading reviews, not yet ready to commit — WebChat keeps them engaged.
  • ServiceTitan-native workflow
    If your CSRs are trained on ServiceTitan, WebChat fits naturally into that workflow during business hours.
  • Non-emergency service requests
    Routine maintenance scheduling from homeowners who prefer a chat interface while browsing options.
