Why SMS First?

Homeowners text. Phone calls go to voicemail. The data is clear: over 90% of homeowners under 45 prefer texting a business over calling. When your HVAC goes out at 11 PM on a Saturday, the homeowner is not pressing "1 for service" on a phone tree — they're texting the first number Google gives them.

The first contractor to reply via text wins 78% of jobs. Maya is SMS-native: no hold music, no "press 1," just an instant qualified conversation that ends with an appointment on your calendar. Phone-first receptionists like Smith.ai and Ruby are great for businesses where calls are the primary channel — but for HVAC, roofing, and plumbing contractors, the leads are increasingly arriving via text first.
