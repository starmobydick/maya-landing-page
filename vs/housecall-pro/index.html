HCP's online booking vs. Maya

HCP's built-in online booking is great — for homeowners who visit your website and fill out a form. That's a specific, deliberate action that requires the homeowner to already know you, find your site, and spend 2 minutes completing a form.

But emergency HVAC and roofing leads don't fill out forms. They text the number from your Google ad. They text a referral's saved contact. Maya captures those leads at the text stage and books them into HCP before they can comparison-shop.
