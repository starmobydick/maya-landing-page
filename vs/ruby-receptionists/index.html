The Real Difference

Three reasons the channel you use matters more than how fast someone picks up.

Ruby answers your phone. Maya answers your texts.

Most emergency home service leads text first. When a homeowner's AC dies at 11 PM, they're texting 3-4 contractors simultaneously — not leaving voicemails. Ruby's phone-first model misses this entire channel.

Ruby charges per minute. Maya charges per booked appointment.

With Ruby, you pay whether the caller is a qualified lead or a wrong number. Maya's pricing is tied to outcomes — you pay when a job gets booked, not when someone asks if you service their city.

Ruby takes messages. Maya books appointments.

A Ruby agent can take a message and relay it to you. Maya books the job directly into ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, or Jobber before the lead even considers calling a competitor.
